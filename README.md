<h2>🎯 What Can I Do?</h2>

- **Data analysis and trend identification:** Analyzing historical, operational, survey, and performance data to uncover patterns, measure outcomes, and support strategic decision-making.

- **Program evaluation and assessment:** Evaluating initiatives, services, and pilot programs to determine effectiveness, identify opportunities for improvement, and measure organizational impact.

- **Institutional and organizational research:** Conducting enrollment, workforce, economic, system, and operational analyses to answer complex questions and support long-term planning.

- **Survey design and stakeholder research:** Developing surveys, designing survey questions, testing instrument validity, analyzing quantitative and qualitative responses, and translating stakeholder feedback into actionable recommendations.

- **Dashboard development and data visualization:** Creating interactive dashboards and visual reports in Power BI and Google Data Studio.

- **Student success and pathway analytics:** Examining student pathways, enrollment trends, academic performance, retention, persistence, graduation, and student outcomes to support evidence-based interventions.

- **Business intelligence and decision support:** Connecting data, systems, and organizational objectives to help leadership make informed, data-driven decisions.

- **Reporting and data storytelling:** Translating technical findings into executive summaries, presentations, and reports tailored to diverse stakeholder audiences.
